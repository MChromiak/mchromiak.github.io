Summary: COLT5 (Conditional LongT5) is based on LongT5 ([T5 Version 1.1](https://github.com/google-research/text-to-text-transfer-transformer/blob/main/released_checkpoints.md#t511)) with more overall parameters (but less compute) due to the conditional computations. CoLT5 achieves SOTA results on 65k input tokens due to the conditional computation in feedforward and attention layers for 'important' tokens. The number of important tokens is not scaling so fast as the length of the input sequence. 

propose optimization LongT5 architecture with an idea that for feedforward and attention layers we can use different number of heads and layers for different tokens. This idea is called conditional computation. The authors of the paper show that this idea can be applied to LongT5 architecture and achieve SOTA results on 65k input tokens.
