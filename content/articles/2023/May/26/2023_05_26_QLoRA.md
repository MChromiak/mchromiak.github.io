Summary: QLoRA is claimed to enable fine-tune a 65B parameter model on a single 48GB GPU preserving 16-bit finetuning task performance. With QLoRA comes _Guanaco_ model family, as a chatbot on a single GPU, achieving **99% ChatGPT performance** on the Vicuna benchmark. <br>  With QLoRA you can fine-tune Guanaco 33B/65B models on a single 24/48GB GPU taking only 12/24h for a finetuning run. QLoRA replicates 16-bit performance in all scenarios and scales tested.
